Nutrition Facts

Here is the approximate nutritional information for one serving of Classic French Ratatouille (based on 6 servings):

  • Calories: 150
  • Fat: 7g
  • Carbohydrates: 20g
  • Fiber: 6g
  • Protein: 3g

Variations

Feel free to experiment with different vegetables or herbs to suit your taste. Here are some ideas:

  • Add mushrooms or spinach for extra nutrients.
  • Incorporate a protein source like chickpeas or tofu for a more filling meal.
  • Use fresh herbs like basil, parsley, or thyme instead of dried ones for a different flavor profile.

Serving Suggestions

Classic French Ratatouille can be enjoyed in various ways:

  1. As a side dish alongside grilled meats or fish.
  2. Over a bed of rice, quinoa, or couscous for a light main course.
  3. With crusty bread to soak up the delicious juices.
